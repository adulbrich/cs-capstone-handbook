# Individual Contribution (Sprint Notes) - Rubrics

This assignment is the individual half of the Sprint Notes points (see the
[Assignments Overview](https://capstone.alexulbrich.com/assignments/introduction/)
and [Sprint Notes](https://capstone.alexulbrich.com/assignments/sprint-notes/)
in the handbook). It **defaults to full credit**; graders only deduct, per
sprint, when the evidence shows no meaningful contribution.

## Structure

The assignment is worth 100 points, split equally across the term's sprints:

- Fall and winter (4 sprint notes): 25 points per sprint.
- Spring (2 sprint notes): 50 points per sprint.

## Per-Sprint Deduction Logic

Each sprint is scored full, half, or zero. Every deduction requires an evidence
note from the grader (what was checked, what was found), so the decision is
reviewable.

- **Full credit** (default): the sprint's contribution line lists reviewed PR(s)
  the student owned, and the repo record corroborates it; or the PR norm was
  missed once with an explanation in the sprint note (normal life, no
  deduction).
- **Half credit**: evidence of some activity, but no owned, reviewed PR and no
  explanation in the note; or the contribution line is present but the repo
  record only weakly corroborates it (e.g., trivial commits, review-only
  activity not agreed as that student's contribution mode in the charter).
- **Zero credit**: no meaningful contribution visible anywhere for the sprint
  (no contribution line, no PRs, no reviews, no traceable work) and no
  explanation, or the contribution line is contradicted by the repo record.

## Evidence Sources

Deductions are evidence-based, never testimonial:

1. Contribution lines in the team's Sprint Notes (PR titles or IDs; Track B
   teams list IDs only).
2. The repository record: authored or driven PRs, review responses, merges.
3. Review activity over the sprint window.
4. Live spot-verification at the Defense.
5. Partner or peer flags trigger the corroboration review described on the
   [partner evaluation page](https://capstone.alexulbrich.com/project-evaluation/project-partner-evaluation/);
   the student can respond with evidence before a deduction is finalized.

A pattern of missed sprints with no explanation is what this modifier exists
for; a single explained miss costs nothing.
