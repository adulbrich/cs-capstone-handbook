Students' final grade in each course is composed of four equal components:

Component | Weight | Description
----------|--------|------------
Project Partner Evaluation | **25%** | Assessment by the project partner or mentor (midterm pulse 5% + final survey 20%), using the [partner rubrics](/project-evaluation/project-partner-evaluation/)
Peer Evaluation | **25%** | Assessment by teammates (midterm survey 5% + final survey 20%), see [peer evaluations](/project-evaluation/peer-evaluations/)
Individual Evidence | **25%** | Individually graded work: the [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) (15%) and [Defense](/assignments/defense/) (10%) in fall and winter; the [Career and Individual Retrospective](/assignments/career-retrospective/) (15%) and Defense (10%) in spring
Team Deliverables | **25%** | The term's team assignments, with the individual contribution modifier; see the per-term tables on the [assignments overview](/assignments/introduction/)

This structure is consistent across all three terms (CS 461, CS 462, CS 463). Half of every student's grade is individually determined (peer evaluation plus individual evidence), and the team half carries individual accountability mechanisms of its own.

## Project Partner Assessment Facets

The project partner evaluates the team on six facets using the rubrics detailed on the [project partner evaluation page](/project-evaluation/project-partner-evaluation/). The emphasis on each facet changes throughout the year to reflect project progression:

Facet | CS461 | CS462 | CS463
------|-------|-------|------
Reflection (learning outcomes, feedback, impact, IP, etc.) | 15% | 15% | 15%
Requirements and Specifications | **40%** | 15% | 5%
Design, Implementation, and Deployment | 15% | **40%** | 20%
Verification and Validation (outcome) | 5% | 10% | **40%**
Teamwork | 15% | 10% | 10%
Communication (abstract, demo, presentation) | 10% | 10% | 10%

The weight migration tells the year's story: fall is won on requirements, winter on building, spring on the shipped outcome, scored against the team's agreed [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/).

## Important Notes

- If students change projects during the series, instructors won't adjust the distribution, and extra effort will likely be needed to meet expectations.
- The facet distribution above applies specifically to **project partner evaluations** (25% of the total grade).
- Every rubric criterion in the assignments section is tagged with the learning outcome it evidences; see the [outcome mapping](/learning-objectives/mapping/).
