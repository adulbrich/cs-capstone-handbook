This page maps every learning outcome to the assignments and rubric criteria that evidence it. Three rules govern the map:

1. **Individual evidence for accreditation.** ABET and WIC outcomes must be evidenced at the individual student level. Team-level instruments (partner evaluations, repo checkpoints, team deliverables) appear only as corroboration.
2. **At least two data points per student per outcome.** A data point is a rubric criterion or an assignment scored per student. Every ABET outcome below clears this bar inside the capstone alone, most with margin.
3. **Program-level context.** ABET assesses the program and accepts representative sampling of student work. Some outcomes also draw data points from other courses; SO4 is partly evidenced by the ethics course (CS 391).

## ABET Student Outcomes

| Outcome | Individual data points (per student, per year) | Team-level corroboration |
|---|---|---|
| **SO1** Analyze a complex computing problem and apply principles of computing to identify solutions | [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) fall and winter: problem framing criterion + options criterion (4 data points); [Defense](/assignments/defense/) each term: judgment criterion (3) | Partner evaluation Requirements facet; [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/) decision and risk hygiene |
| **SO2** Design, implement, and evaluate a computing-based solution | RFC fall and winter: recommendation and verification plan criterion (2); Defense each term: ownership criterion (3); [Career Retrospective](/assignments/career-retrospective/): PR portfolio criterion (1) | Partner evaluation Design and V&V facets; repo checkpoints; [Release and Metrics](/assignments/release/) |
| **SO3** Communicate effectively in a variety of professional contexts | RFC fall and winter: writing, revision, and peer feedback criteria (6); Defense each term: communication criterion (3); Career Retrospective: writing criterion (1) | Demos, [landing page](/assignments/landing-page/), [handoff](/assignments/project-handoff/), Expo |
| **SO4** Recognize professional responsibilities and make informed judgments based on legal and ethical principles | Ethics course (CS 391, program-level data point); RFC fall and winter: constraints criterion + AI disclosure criterion (4); Career Retrospective: responsible practice criterion (1). Known concentration: 4 of 5 in-capstone points come from the RFC; diversifying is on the roadmap | Team Charter AI and confidentiality section; blameless conduct in the [postmortem](/assignments/incident-postmortem/) |
| **SO5** Function effectively as a member or leader of a team | [Peer evaluations](/project-evaluation/peer-evaluations/), midterm and final, every term (6, the spring final includes the CATME instrument); Defense each term: role accountability criterion (3) | Partner evaluation Teamwork facet; sprint note contribution traceability; charter roles |
| **SO6** Apply computer science theory and software development fundamentals | RFC fall and winter: technical tradeoff criterion (2); Defense each term: technical depth criterion (3) | Repo checkpoint build health; postmortem root cause analysis |

Every outcome has at least five individual data points per student per year; the two-point minimum holds even if a defense round or an RFC is missed.

## WIC Outcomes (CS 462)

The WIC requirements are under official revision; this mapping will be finalized against the new language. The current structure already carries:

| Outcome | Where |
|---|---|
| **L07** Frequent informal and formal writing | RFC drafts and reviews (formal), sprint note judgment layers (informal, ~10 per year), retrospective individual pages |
| **L08** Audience, genre, and convention awareness | RFC (engineering decision genre; the writing criterion assesses this individually), release notes and landing page (public genre, team-level corroboration) |
| **L09** 2,000+ word document through drafting, sources, and comprehensive revision after feedback | The two RFCs: each runs a full draft, cross-team feedback, revision cycle with a response-to-feedback note; combined polished output exceeds 2,000 words per student |

## How This Stays True

Every assignment page carries outcome tags in its rubric table and declares matching counts in frontmatter. `scripts/validate-outcomes.mjs` (run in CI and pre-commit) parses the rubric tables directly, fails if frontmatter and rubric disagree, fails if any ABET outcome drops below two individual data points, and fails if any WIC or Beyond OSU outcome loses individual coverage. This page is hand-maintained against that check.
