You want to know what letter your term's points earn and what that letter says about your work. A capstone project should:

- exemplify the learning objectives;
- demonstrate the skills expected of a graduate;
- make appropriate trade-offs and judgments; and
- not suffer serious oversights.

A central premise is that the software does what it was built to do, as one working system rather than separate parts.

Here's how points convert to letter grades. The `Points` column is the minimum for the grade: 93 points or more earns an `A`, strictly less than 60 points is an `F`.

Letter Grade | Points | Description
-------------|--------|------------
A  | 93 | demonstrates mastery on all learning objectives, with no oversights
A- | 90 | demonstrates mastery on most learning objectives, with some minor oversights
B+ | 87 | adequacy on all learning objectives, mastery of several, with some minor oversights
B  | 83 | adequacy on all learning objectives, with some minor oversights
B- | 80 | adequacy on all learning objectives, with some serious oversights
C+ | 77 | adequacy on most learning objectives, minor oversights elsewhere
C  | 73 | adequacy on most learning objectives, with some serious oversights
C- | 70 | adequacy on several learning objectives, with serious oversights
D+ | 67 | significant shortcomings on learning objectives
D  | 60 | significant shortcomings in learning objectives, major oversights
F  | < 60| unethical, unprofessional, incomplete, etc.

Every letter is defined against the [learning objectives](/learning-objectives/mapping/), not against effort or volume. A project that consumed a term of work and demonstrates adequacy on most objectives is a C, and a smaller project that demonstrates mastery on all of them is an A.

## Learning Outcome Tags

Each rubric criterion in the [assignments section](/assignments/introduction/) carries a tag: `SO1` through `SO6` for the [ABET student outcomes](/learning-objectives/abet/), `L07` through `L09` for [WIC](/learning-objectives/wic/), and `L10` for [Beyond OSU](/learning-objectives/beyond-osu/).

The tags show accreditation reviewers which outcome each criterion evidences, and change nothing about how you complete the work. The [outcome mapping](/learning-objectives/mapping/) shows where every outcome is evidenced.

Where rubric bands come from and how missing work is scored is on the [assignments overview](/assignments/introduction/#rubric-conventions).
