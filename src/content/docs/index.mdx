<h2 id="what-students-learn" class="text-center">What Students Learn</h2>

<p>Students take one project from a problem statement to a released artifact with real users, backed by <a href="/guides/introduction/">engineering guides</a> on requirements, design, testing, security, and deployment. Each student is assessed on four things partners hire for: <span class="font-bold">critical thinking</span>, arguing a hard technical decision in writing and defending it live; <span class="font-bold">AI literacy</span>, knowing how far to delegate and what safety net makes that reasonable; <span class="font-bold">leadership</span>, measured by decisions driven to a conclusion; and <span class="font-bold">collaboration</span>, proven across three terms on the same team.</p>

<h2 id="partner-with-us" class="text-center">Partner with Us</h2>

<p class="text-center">Companies, faculty, and founders bring the projects. A project costs you about an hour a week and an evaluation each term.</p>
