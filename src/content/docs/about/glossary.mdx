One word per thing, everywhere on this site. Each entry names the page that owns the rule. A "Not:" list is what the prose check rejects in the handbook, the syllabi and rubric files, and the staff runbook.

## People

- **Project partner**: the person or organization the team builds for and who completes the [partner evaluations](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/); for a student-proposed project, the assigned mentor or the instructor acting as mentor ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/#how-you-get-your-project-and-team)). Not: client, sponsor.
- **Mentor**: a professional or faculty member who advises a team; every team that does not arrive with a project partner needs one ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/#how-you-get-your-project-and-team)), and a mentor's evaluation carries a project partner's weight.
- **TA**: a Teaching Assistant, written "Teaching Assistant (TA)" at first use on a page and TA after; each TA owns a set of teams and runs their [TA check-ins](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins).
- **Instructor**: the faculty running the course; instructors place students on teams ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/#how-you-get-your-project-and-team)) and run the [resume meetings](/assignments/resume-and-intent/).
- **The instruction team**: instructors and TAs together, the handbook's third-person name for the people who run the course. Not: teaching staff, course staff, teaching team, instruction staff.
- **The class**: every student in the course this year ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/)). Not: cohort.

## Rhythm

- **Term and week**: how this site tells time, "fall, week 5" ([Schedule](/introduction/schedule/)).
- **Sprint**: the fixed development cycle, each ending in a [sprint note](/assignments/sprint-notes/) and a demo. Not: sprint report, progress report.
- **Stand-up**: the team's short recurring sync ([Regular Stand-Up Meetings](/activities/teamwork/#regular-stand-up-meetings)). Not: standup.
- **TA check-in**: the standing meeting between a team and its TA in every sprint-note week, where the team demos ([Assignments Overview](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins)). Not: cohort check-in.
- **Demo day**: the all-hands demo session; presenting earns extra credit, and every team takes one of the five across the academic year ([Demo Day](/assignments/demo-day/)). The [sprint demo](/assignments/sprint-notes/#demo-cadence) a team gives in its own TA check-in is a different session.
- **Resume meeting**: the meeting with an instructor in fall and again in spring ([Resume and Intent](/assignments/resume-and-intent/), [Career and Individual Retrospective](/assignments/career-retrospective/)). Not: cohort meeting.
- **Defense**: the once-a-term live session where each student explains work they own ([Defense](/assignments/defense/)).
- **Expo**: the spring showcase where teams present to the public ([Engineering Expo](/assignments/expo/)).

## Work and its evidence

- **Living docs**: the documents the repository must contain, kept current, and submitted at each checkpoint as a PDF matching a named commit ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#what-the-repository-must-contain-the-living-docs) lists them).
- **Repo checkpoint** and **term gate**: the instruction team's review of the repository as it is, each carrying one gate ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#term-gates)).
- **Walking skeleton** and **"hello, production"**: the thinnest end-to-end slice of the system, deployed for the fall production gate ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#term-gates)).
- **Definition of Shipped** and **target rung**: the winter agreement with the project partner about what "shipped" means for this project, naming the rung of the outcome ladder the team commits to ([Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/)). Not: outcome type, project type, project category.
- **Outcome ladder**: the one spring scale for Verification and Validation, who ran the software ([Project Partner Evaluation](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/#spring)). Not: V&V ladder, category ladder.
- **Individual contribution modifier**: the per-student adjustment on sprint note points when the evidence falls short of the contribution floor ([Assignments Overview](/assignments/introduction/#individual-accountability-on-team-work)).
- **Midterm Pulse** and **End-of-Term Survey**: the two partner surveys each term ([Project Partner Evaluation](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/)).
- **Peer evaluation**: the per-term survey students complete about teammates; **CATME** is the additional instrument at the end of spring ([Peer Evaluations](/assignments/peer-evaluations/)).
- **Inherited-codebase audit**: the write-up a team produces when it starts in an existing codebase, checked at the fall week 5 gate ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#the-inherited-codebase-audit)).

## Projects

- **Existing codebase**: a predecessor team's repository, a partner's codebase, or an upstream FOSS project; one situation with three origins ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#the-inherited-codebase-audit)). Not: legacy project.
- **Student-proposed project**: a project a student submitted through the portal, with a mentor or partner attached ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/#how-you-get-your-project-and-team)). Not: student-driven.
- **Multi-team project**: one project with several teams, split negotiated with the project partner in week 1 and written into each team's [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/).
- **NDA project**: a project whose agreement restricts what the instruction team or the public may see; each affected assignment page carries a short note ([IP, NDA, and Licensing](/introduction/ip-nda-and-licensing/)). Not: Track A, Track B.
