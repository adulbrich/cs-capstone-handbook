One word per thing, everywhere on this site. Each entry names the page that owns the rule. A "Not:" list is what the prose check rejects in the handbook, the Canvas files, and the staff runbook.

## People

- **Project partner**: the person or organization the team builds for and who completes the [partner evaluations](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/); for a student-proposed project, the assigned mentor or the instructor acting as mentor ([Projects and Teams](/practicalities/projects-and-teams/)). Not: client, sponsor.
- **Mentor**: a professional or faculty member who advises a team; [Project Types](/practicalities/types/) says which outcome types require one.
- **TA**: a Teaching Assistant, written "Teaching Assistant (TA)" at first use on a page and TA after; each TA owns a set of teams and runs their [TA check-ins](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins).
- **Instructor**: the faculty running the course; the instructors place students on teams ([Projects and Teams](/practicalities/projects-and-teams/#how-teams-form)) and run the [resume meetings](/assignments/resume-and-intent/).
- **The class**: every student in the course this year ([For Students](/introduction/for-students/)). Not: cohort.

## Rhythm

- **Term and week**: how this site tells time, "fall, week 5" ([Schedule](/introduction/schedule/)).
- **Sprint**: the fixed development cycle, each ending in a [sprint note](/assignments/sprint-notes/) and a demo. Not: sprint report, progress report.
- **Stand-up**: the team's short recurring sync ([Regular Stand-Up Meetings](/activities/teamwork/#regular-stand-up-meetings)). Not: standup.
- **TA check-in**: the standing meeting between a team and its TA in every sprint-note week, where the team demos ([Assignments Overview](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins)). Not: cohort check-in.
- **Demo day**: the all-hands demo session, not assessed; the [schedule](/introduction/schedule/) has the weeks and [Sprint Notes](/assignments/sprint-notes/) the format.
- **Resume meeting**: the small-group meeting with an instructor in fall and again in spring ([Resume and Intent](/assignments/resume-and-intent/), [Career and Individual Retrospective](/assignments/career-retrospective/)). Not: cohort meeting.
- **Defense**: the once-a-term live session where each student explains work they own ([Defense](/assignments/defense/)).
- **Expo**: the spring showcase where teams present to the public ([Expo](/practicalities/expo/)).

## Work and its evidence

- **Living docs**: the documents the repository must contain, kept current and graded in place ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#what-the-repository-must-contain-the-living-docs) lists them).
- **Repo checkpoint** and **term gate**: the staff review of the repository as it is, each carrying one gate ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#term-gates)).
- **Walking skeleton** and **"hello, production"**: the thinnest end-to-end slice of the system, deployed for the fall production gate ([Repo Checkpoints](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/#term-gates)).
- **Definition of Shipped** and **target rung**: the winter agreement with the project partner about what "shipped" means for this project, naming the rung of the outcome ladder the team commits to ([Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/)).
- **Outcome ladder**: the one spring scale for Verification and Validation, who ran the software ([Project Partner Evaluation](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/#spring)). Not: V&V ladder, category ladder.
- **Individual contribution modifier**: the per-student adjustment on sprint note points when the evidence shows no meaningful contribution ([Assignments Overview](/assignments/introduction/#individual-accountability-on-team-work)).
- **Midterm Pulse** and **Final Evaluation**: the two partner surveys each term ([Project Partner Evaluation](/assignments/project-partner-evaluation/)).
- **Peer evaluation**: the per-term survey students complete about teammates; **CATME** is the additional instrument at the end of spring ([Peer Evaluations](/assignments/peer-evaluations/)).
- **Inherited-codebase audit**: the write-up a team produces when it starts in an existing codebase, checked at the fall week 5 gate ([Project Types](/practicalities/types/#where-the-code-starts)).

## Projects

- **Project type**: any trait on the [Project Types](/practicalities/types/) page; a project has several at once. Not: project category.
- **Outcome type**: FOSS, Research, Consultancy, or New Product or Game, agreed by the team and the project partner and used only to read the outcome ladder and pick the shipping playbook; not every project is one of the four ([Project Types](/practicalities/types/#who-runs-the-result-the-outcome-type)). Not: project categories.
- **Existing codebase**: a predecessor team's repository, a partner's codebase, or an upstream FOSS project; one situation with three origins ([Project Types](/practicalities/types/#where-the-code-starts)). Not: legacy project.
- **Student-proposed project**: a project a student submitted through the portal, with a mentor or partner attached ([Projects and Teams](/practicalities/projects-and-teams/)). Not: student-driven.
- **Multi-team project**: one project with several teams, split negotiated with the project partner in week 1 ([Project Types](/practicalities/types/#where-it-comes-from)).
- **NDA project**: a project whose agreement restricts what staff or the public may see; each affected assignment page carries a short note ([IP, NDA, and Licensing](/practicalities/projects-and-teams/#ip-nda-and-licensing)). Not: Track A, Track B.

## Activities

- **Workshop**, **Recommended**, **library**: the three activity tiers ([Practice Library](/activities/introduction/)); Workshops run in class ([Workshop Activities](/assignments/workshop-activities/)).
- **Feeds line**: the last line of a Workshop or Recommended activity, naming the assignment criterion it prepares ([Practice Library](/activities/introduction/)).
