The guides are the engineering handbook inside the handbook: opinionated, concrete references for how to run a real software project. They are not assignments; they are how you get good at the things the assignments assess. Reach for them when your team hits the corresponding problem, or better, one sprint before.

## Working Together

- [Working Agreement](/guides/working-agreement/): roles, decision-making, conflict handling, and AI tool norms, written down before you need them.
- [Sprint](/guides/sprint/): the two-week cadence, the four ceremonies, and what "done" has to mean for them to work.
- [Planning and Prioritization](/guides/planning/): ideation techniques, prioritization methods, and how to pick a success metric you can actually measure.
- [Retrospectives](/guides/retrospectives/): formats and facilitation for term retrospectives and beyond.
- [Conflict and Performance Conversations](/guides/conflict/): conflict-handling modes, raising a contribution problem, and when to escalate.

## Building

- [Git and GitHub](/guides/git-and-github/): branching, reviews, and repository discipline, including the PR-per-sprint ownership norm.
- [Requirements](/guides/requirements/): writing testable requirements for your repo's living `docs/requirements.md`.
- [Technical Design](/guides/technical-design/): the system view in `docs/design.md`, and why design docs matter more, not less, in the AI era.
- [ADR](/guides/adr/): recording a decision and its reasoning, so the argument survives the people who had it.
- [Generative AI](/guides/generative-ai/): where to spend attention, the safety net that covers the rest, and team coordination.
- [AI Project Setup](/guides/ai-project-setup/): `AGENTS.md` and its per-tool equivalents, MCP servers, and skills, all checked into the repository.

## Verifying and Shipping

- [Testing Strategy](/guides/testing-strategy/): the pyramid, contracts, and what to test when time is short.
- [Accessibility Testing](/guides/accessibility/): the automated gates, the keyboard and screen-reader pass, and testing with a daily assistive-technology user.
- [Security](/guides/security/): STRIDE, secrets, dependencies, and managed auth.
- [DevOps](/guides/devops/): CI/CD, environments, deployment platforms, and rollbacks.
- [Shipping](/guides/shipping/): the path for each outcome type from prototype to the top of the outcome ladder, and the external clocks to start early.
- [Documentation](/guides/documentation/): the living docs your repo must carry and how to keep them honest.

## Communicating

- [Presentations](/guides/presentations/): sprint demos, stakeholder presentations, design reviews, and public demonstrations such as the Expo.
