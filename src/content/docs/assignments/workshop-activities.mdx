In this assignment your team runs an activity from the [practice library](/activities/introduction/) in class and submits its output. Each workshop below is its own entry, with the activity to run, what a complete submission holds, and the later work it makes easier. The more than a hundred other activities in the library are optional.

## Fall

### Workshop 1: User Story Mapping

- **Activity:** [User Story Mapping](/activities/requirements/#user-story-mapping)
- **A complete submission:** a user story map of your project's main user flows, with epics and written user stories, each marked in or out of scope for this year
- **What it makes easier:** `docs/requirements.md` at every [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/), in the shape the [requirements guide](/guides/requirements/#story-mapping) sets out; the winter [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/)

### Workshop 2: Map Your Hard-to-Reverse Decisions

- **Activity:** [Map Your Hard-to-Reverse Decisions](/activities/ai/#map-your-hard-to-reverse-decisions)
- **A complete submission:** the list of upcoming decisions with the few hard-to-reverse ones marked
- **What it makes easier:** your [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) topic, which has to be a decision that is hard to reverse; the [defense](/assignments/defense/) judgment question

This workshop shares its class session with Workshop 3, and each has its own submission. Both land before the RFC draft at the end of week 4, and the hard-to-reverse decision you pick here is usually the RFC topic. [Describe your Architecture](/activities/design/#describe-your-architecture) is recommended the same week and takes an hour or two on your own; it feeds `docs/design.md` and the RFC framing.

### Workshop 3: Audit Your Safety Net

- **Activity:** [Audit Your Safety Net](/activities/ai/#audit-your-safety-net)
- **A complete submission:** one specific named gap in your checks and the smallest change that closes it
- **What it makes easier:** four criteria across the checkpoints, the RFC, and the defense

This workshop runs in the same class session as Workshop 2.

### Workshop 4: Peer Technical Design Review

- **Activity:** [Peer Technical Design Review](/activities/design/#peer-technical-design-review)
- **A complete submission:** your one-page design, the findings the other team gave you, and each finding marked accepted or declined with a reason
- **What it makes easier:** the [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) final, due the week after: its revision criterion, and the [defense](/assignments/defense/) technical-depth question

Pair with the team at the next table for the whole session; with an odd number of teams, one group of three reviews in a ring. Nothing is prepared before class.

:::note[If your project is under NDA]
The other team sees your design page, so describe components by their role (a message queue, a model server) and leave out the partner's specifics.
:::

### Workshop 5: Identify Success Metrics

- **Activity:** [Identify Success Metrics](/activities/planning/#identify-success-metrics)
- **A complete submission:** 2 or 3 metrics with their decision rules and a named instrumentation method for at least one, dated
- **What it makes easier:** the v0 [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/) draft at the week 10 [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/), and the spring [Release and Metrics](/assignments/release/): the number that proves it worked, chosen while there is still time to instrument it

## Winter

### Workshop 1: Dependency Mapping or Critical Path Analysis

- **Activity:** [Dependency Mapping or Critical Path Analysis](/activities/planning/#dependency-mapping-or-critical-path-analysis)
- **A complete submission:** a dependency map and critical path analysis, with the bottlenecks and the adjustments you made
- **What it makes easier:** the [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/), due two weeks later: the external clocks named and started

### Workshop 2: Team Dysfunctions Assessment

- **Activity:** [Team Dysfunctions Assessment](/activities/teamwork/#team-dysfunctions-assessment)
- **A complete submission:** the dysfunctions you identified, a strategy for each, and a reflection on how they will improve the team
- **What it makes easier:** the midterm [peer evaluation](/assignments/peer-evaluations/) in week 5: naming a problem before the survey does; the [defense](/assignments/defense/) role question later

### Workshop 3: Test Plan

- **Activity:** [Test Plan](/activities/design/#test-plan)
- **A complete submission:** a test plan for the paths that must work at the release candidate, with their test cases, methods, and pass criteria
- **What it makes easier:** the week-5 [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/): the tests in CI that must exist before real users arrive at the release candidate

## Spring

### Workshop 1: Team Health Assessment

- **Activity:** [Team Health Assessment](/activities/teamwork/#team-health-assessment)
- **A complete submission:** your completed health check with its two lowest dimensions carried into your next retrospective, plus the charter refresh below
- **What it makes easier:** the spring [charter](/assignments/team-charter/) refresh and the midterm [peer evaluation](/assignments/peer-evaluations/); the [Project Retrospective](/assignments/project-retrospective/) team-dynamics criterion later

The assessment closes with the charter refresh: update the charter (the [working agreement guide](/guides/working-agreement/) has the patterns) with what it surfaced (roles for the final term, the decision process, anything the winter postmortem changed); a change to the Definition of Done or the Contribution Norm goes in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## What You Submit

The workshop's complete submission, as its section above describes, in one PDF ([Submitting Your Work](/assignments/introduction/#submitting-your-work)); a photo of a whiteboard or sticky notes goes inside the PDF.

## Rubric (100 points per workshop)

<RubricTable csv={rubricCsv} sourceLabel="canvas/assignments/workshop-activities/workshop-activities-rubric.csv" />

Do the workshop in class, or by the end of the week the class happened, for credit; a workshop submitted after that week earns no points ([late work](/assignments/introduction/#late-work-absence-and-makeup)).

A submission is complete when it is the activity's output; there is no quality bar.

**AI use:** use AI tools the way the activity page describes. Submitting an AI-generated output without running the activity as a team scores Incomplete.

:::note[If your project is under NDA]
Submit a sanitized version: what you did, without partner-confidential specifics. If that isn't possible, contact the instruction team.
:::
