Nine activities from the [practice library](/activities/introduction/) are run as workshops in class: five in fall, three in winter, and one in spring. Each session runs the activity twice: once by hand, then once with an AI agent. Both outputs go in the same submission, and the differences between them are the point. They are graded, but only on whether you did them: there is no quality bar, no bands, and no rubric. Submit the output and you get the points.

Each one makes several later rubric criteria easier; the tables below name which. The more than a hundred other activities in the [library](/activities/introduction/) are optional.

## Fall (5 items, 2%)

| Activity | Week | What it makes easier |
|---|---|---|
| [User Story Mapping](/activities/requirements/#user-story-mapping) ([what it is](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-story-mapping/)) | 2 | `docs/requirements.md` at every [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/), in the shape the [requirements guide](/guides/requirements/#story-mapping) sets out; the winter [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/) |
| [Map Your One-Way Doors](/activities/ai/#map-your-one-way-doors) | 3 | Your [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) topic, which has to be a decision that is hard to reverse; the [defense](/assignments/defense/) judgment question |
| [Audit Your Safety Net](/activities/ai/#audit-your-safety-net) | 3 | Four criteria across the checkpoints, the RFC, and the defense |
| [Peer Technical Design Review](/activities/design/#peer-technical-design-review) | 7 | The [RFC](/assignments/rfc/) final, due the week after: its feedback-given and revision criteria, and the [defense](/assignments/defense/) technical-depth question |
| [Identify Success Metrics](/activities/planning/#identify-success-metrics) | 8 | The v0 [Definition of Shipped](/assignments/definition-of-shipped/) draft at the week 10 [repo checkpoint](/assignments/repo-checkpoints/), and the spring [Release and Metrics](/assignments/release/): the number that proves it worked, chosen while there is still time to instrument it |

Week 3 is one session with two outputs: the decisions you cannot walk back, and the net that catches everything else. Both land before the RFC draft at the end of week 4, and the one-way door you pick is usually the RFC topic. [Describe your Architecture](/activities/design/#describe-your-architecture) is recommended the same week and takes an hour or two on your own; it feeds `docs/design.md` and the RFC framing.

|---|---|---|

| [Team Dysfunctions Assessment](/activities/teamwork/#team-dysfunctions-assessment) | 2 | The midterm [peer evaluation](/assignments/peer-evaluations/) in week 5: naming a problem before the survey does; the [defense](/assignments/defense/) role question later |

## Spring (1 item, 1%)

| Activity | Week | What it makes easier |
|---|---|---|
| [Team Health Assessment](/activities/teamwork/#team-health-assessment) | 2 | The spring [charter](/assignments/team-charter/) refresh and the midterm [peer evaluation](/assignments/peer-evaluations/); the [Project Retrospective](/assignments/project-retrospective/) team-dynamics criterion later |

The spring assessment closes with the charter refresh: update `docs/charter.md` (the [working agreement guide](/guides/working-agreement/) has the patterns) with what it surfaced (roles for the final term, the decision process, anything the winter postmortem changed) and commit it. Spring has no repo checkpoint to carry the refresh, so this workshop is where it lives.

The teamwork slot changes instrument between winter and spring, so it tells you something new rather than asking you to retake the same survey.

## Grading

One Canvas column per term, scored as complete/incomplete per item: 5 checkboxes in fall, 3 in winter, 1 in spring. The spring week-6 Resume Building session runs like a workshop but is not scored here. Submit the activity's output, whatever the activity says a good output is. A grader spends seconds per item confirming something real was submitted.

There is no partial credit within an item and no quality judgment. An output that is obviously not the activity (a blank file, an unrelated document) does not count, but a genuine attempt always does.

**Late work:** these are workshop activities tied to a week, so the standard [late policy](/assignments/introduction/#late-work-absence-and-makeup) applies with one relaxation: a workshop item submitted any time before the end of the term still earns its points. Doing it late costs you the benefit, not the mark.

**Missed the workshop session?** The activity pages are public and self-contained. Read the page, do the activity, submit it.

:::note[NDA teams]
Every workshop activity produces output that stays inside your repository or your team's own documents. Submit a sanitized note confirming what you did, or tell your Teaching Assistant (TA) and cover it in your next check-in. No partner-confidential material needs to leave your boundary for these.
:::
