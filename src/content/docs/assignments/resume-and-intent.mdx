Most capstone resume entries are written in the last week of spring, from memory, and read like a course description. This assignment moves the writing to week 2, when you can still choose what the project will let you claim. You submit the resume you have today and three lines you intend to be able to write by the end of spring, each tied to work the project will actually require of you. An instructor then meets you and seven other students for thirty minutes to react to the lines. In spring, the retrospective asks what happened to each one.

The failure mode this kills is a year of work that produced nothing an interviewer can ask about, discovered at the Expo.

## What You Must Produce (one PDF and one text box, in Canvas)

1. **Your current resume**, as a PDF. Whatever state it is in; it is not graded on quality.
2. **Three intent lines**, in the Canvas text box. Each names the resume line you intend to write by spring week 10 and the project work that will earn it: "By spring week 10 my resume says: ... It will be true because: ...". A technology alone ("learned React") is not project work; name the thing you will have built, shipped, measured, or run.
3. **Your cohort meeting.** Cohorts of eight, thirty minutes, with an instructor, in weeks 3 to 5; your slot is published in Canvas in week 2. Bring the three lines. You leave with them sharper or replaced. There is no written feedback; the meeting is the feedback.

## Rubric (100 points)

Each criterion is all or nothing.

| Criterion | Points | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Resume attached: a PDF, yours, readable | 30 | |
| Three intent lines, each naming a resume line and the project work that earns it | 40 | L10 |
| Cohort meeting attended, with the three lines in hand | 30 | |

Grading notes, in rubric order:

- Resume: any readable PDF that is a resume passes.
- Intent lines: three lines, each with both halves. A line missing either half, or naming only a technology, fails the criterion.
- Meeting: the instructor's attendance record. An absence handled under the [absence policy](/assignments/introduction/#late-work-absence-and-makeup) and rescheduled passes.

**AI use:** Using a model to tighten resume wording or to test whether an intent line is specific is fine. Letting it write the lines from the project description is not: lines that describe the project instead of your part in it fail the criterion, and in spring you will have to show they came true.
