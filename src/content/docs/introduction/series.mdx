Senior Software Engineering Project I, II, and III, collectively known as **Capstone**, represent the culmination of our Computer Science students' academic journey.

Term   | ID       | Class Name | Credits
-------| ---------|------------|--------
Fall   | CS 461   | Senior Software Engineering Project I   | 3
Winter | CS 462   | Senior Software Engineering Project II  | 3
Spring | CS 463   | Senior Software Engineering Project III | 2

This three-course sequence transforms aspiring developers into industry-ready software engineers through real-world project experience and professional collaboration.

Students work in [teams](/practicalities/projects-and-teams/) to design, develop, and deliver software solutions for real-world clients, gaining hands-on experience that bridges the gap between academic learning and professional practice.

## How We Measure Success

Each term, the grade combines four equal components: your project partner's evaluation, your teammates' peer evaluations, individual evidence that nobody can produce for you, and the team's deliverables. The [assignments overview](/assignments/introduction/#grade-architecture) has the breakdown, and the emphasis evolves across the series to mirror how a real project progresses.

## Schedule

Week by week, term by term. Fall has a **week 0** because the term starts midweek; winter and spring start at week 1. Sprints are two weeks long everywhere. Your cohort [check-in](/assignments/introduction/#cohort-check-ins) with your TA falls in every sprint-note week, on the day your TA sets, plus fall week 2 and spring weeks 8 and 10; the defense is a separate session in week 7 or 9.

The **Read** and **Do** columns run about one sprint ahead of what they prepare. The assignment pages are the [source of truth](/assignments/introduction/) for what is due, when, and how it is scored; this schedule only lays them out in time. A Friday marked **None scheduled** has no lecture unless Canvas announces one (a guest speaker, a review round). Every workshop runs in the first hour and closes with the same exercise done with an AI agent.
