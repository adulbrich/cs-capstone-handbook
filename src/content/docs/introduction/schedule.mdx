Fall has a **week 0** because the term starts midweek; winter and spring start at week 1. Sprints run two weeks in every term, and their numbers continue from one term to the next. A label beside each group of weeks names its sprint, or Term Start and Project Handoff for the weeks before the year's first sprint and after its last.

The [assignment pages](/assignments/introduction/) say what is due and how it is scored; this page lays them out in time. A tag on each item due names who submits it: your team, you individually, or your project partner, whose evaluation of the team closes that week. **Read** points at the [guides](/guides/introduction/) that help that week. **Optional** points at [activities](/activities/introduction/) from the practice library. A week with no **In class** line has no class session unless an announcement says otherwise.

Weeks marked <Badge text="TA check-in" variant="tip" /> carry your team's [check-in](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins), at a time your team and your TA agree; in a sprint-note week it includes the [sprint demo](/assignments/sprint-notes/#demo-cadence).
