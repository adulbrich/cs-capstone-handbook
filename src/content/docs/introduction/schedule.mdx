Fall has a **week 0** because the term starts midweek; winter and spring start at week 1. Sprints are two weeks long in every term.

The [assignment pages](/assignments/introduction/) are the source of truth for what is due and how it is scored; this page only lays them out in time. **Read** points at the [guides](/guides/introduction/) that help that week. **Recommended** points at optional [activities](/activities/introduction/) from the practice library. A week with no **Lecture** line has no class session unless Canvas announces one.

Weeks marked <Badge text="TA check-in" variant="tip" /> carry your team's [check-in](/assignments/introduction/#ta-check-ins) and the [sprint demo](/assignments/sprint-notes/#demo-cadence) that goes with it, at a time your team and your TA agree.
